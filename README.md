## About me

I’m Aarav, a CSE AIML student at **SRM Institute of Science and Technology, Kattankulathur**. I build full-stack web products, experiment with AI, and care about making interfaces feel fast, clear, and genuinely useful.

- Currently learning **Next.js, Java, and NestJS**
- Building products around **AI, education, and digital trust**
- Comfortable with **Python, JavaScript, and TypeScript**
- 3+ years of experience editing with **Premiere Pro and After Effects**
- Based in **Kattankulathur, Tamil Nadu**

## Featured projects

### [TrustOS](https://github.com/coderaarav12/Hackathon-TrustOS)

An explainable AI integrity platform for interviews, coding assessments, online examinations, and remote work. TrustOS combines browser signals, behavioral tracking, and multi-agent analysis to produce evidence-backed trust scores.

`Python` `FastAPI` `TypeScript` `React` `Computer Vision` `AI`

### [edutechsrm](https://edutechsrm.in)

A student-built academic dashboard for SRMIST KTR. It brings timetable, attendance, marks, CGPA, assignments, mess menus, notifications, and an AI assistant into one fast interface.

`Next.js` `TypeScript` `Tailwind CSS` `Cloudflare Workers` `AI`

### [MediaHub](https://github.com/coderaarav12/media-hub-frontend)

A media discovery and streaming interface for movies, TV shows, and anime, backed by multiple embed sources.

`JavaScript` `React` `API integration` `Responsive UI`
